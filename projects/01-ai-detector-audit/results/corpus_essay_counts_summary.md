# Essay counts by corpus

`corpus_essay_counts.csv`. Do not edit by hand.

**Small-cell suppression.** Any count below 11 essays, in the cross-tabulation or in the totals below, is shown as `<11`. Where a suppressed count could still be recovered by subtracting other published counts, one more count is hidden and shown as `suppressed` (its true value is 11 or more). Cells with zero essays are not listed. In this release, 139 of 364 cross-tabulation cells are shown as `<11` and 3 as `suppressed`.
